The House

Mont Blanc has been making fine writing instruments since 1906 — and approaches perfumery with the same quiet precision. Legend Blue, launched in 2024 as a sequel to the original Legend, became one of the most-talked-about designer releases of the past two years.

How to wear

Two to three sprays — chest, behind the ears, one wrist. The cool top notes settle within fifteen minutes; the warm cedar-ambroxan heart is where Legend Blue lives.
