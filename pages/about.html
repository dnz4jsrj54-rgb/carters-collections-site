The House

It started at home.

Carter's Collections began as a personal collection — fragrances my wife and I had quietly fallen in love with, kept on a shelf at home and reached for nearly every day.

Our Story

From our shelf to yours.

It started simply. My wife and I had a handful of bottles we genuinely loved — designer icons, a few niche picks, and the occasional viral discovery — and we wore them every day. After enough compliments from friends, strangers in line at the coffee shop, the woman behind us at a wedding — we realized people kept asking the same question. What are you wearing?

So we decided to share them. Not all of them — only the ones we actually reach for. And only at prices that make sense, because we know what these bottles cost at the counter and we know what they're worth.

Carter's Collections is, at its core, the same shelf — just bigger. Every fragrance here is one we wear, or one we'd give to someone we love.

The Curation

Bottles we actually wear.

Every fragrance on the shelf has been tested the slow way — on skin, in heat and cold, day and night, against the dozen other bottles we've worn the same week. The ones that kept getting compliments stayed. The ones we forgot about in three days didn't.

That's the only test. No algorithm, no buyer's meeting, no trend deck. Just whether we'd reach for it tomorrow morning, and whether we'd hand it to a friend.

The Prices

The best we can offer.

Designer fragrances are marked up dramatically at the counter — sometimes three to four times their real cost. We source through the same authorized channels and pass as much of that savings on to you as we can while still keeping the lights on.

Every bottle is one hundred percent authentic, factory-sealed, and shipped from Austin in a Carter's box. We don't sell what we wouldn't wear, and we don't price what we wouldn't pay.
