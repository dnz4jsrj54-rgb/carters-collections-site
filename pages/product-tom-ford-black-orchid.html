The House

Tom Ford launched his eponymous house in 2006 with Black Orchid as the debut fragrance — a deliberate provocation against the lighter florals dominating women's perfumery at the time. Two decades later, it remains his most iconic bottle.

How to wear

One to two sprays — chest only. Black Orchid is famously bold; over-application turns it heavy. Wear it on nights when you want to be remembered. Pairs beautifully with velvet and silk.
