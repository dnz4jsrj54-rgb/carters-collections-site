The House

Lattafa is a Sharjah-based house that has built its reputation on traditional Middle Eastern perfumery — rose, oud, amber, and saffron — delivered at a price that rewards real wear. His Confession is one of their best-loved compositions: an amber-leather signature that holds its own against fragrances at four times the price.

How to wear

Apply two sprays to clean, lightly moisturized pulse points — the inside of the wrists, just behind the ears, the base of the neck. Avoid rubbing the wrists together; let the heat of the skin lift the heart notes on its own.
