The House

Jean Paul Gaultier's Le Male has been one of the most recognized men's fragrances in the world for over thirty years. Le Parfum is the heavier, more intimate counterpart — built on the same cardamom-lavender-vanilla DNA, deepened with iris and tonka. A signature that doesn't need a second introduction.

How to wear

Two to three sprays on pulse points — the inside of the wrists, the base of the neck, the chest. Avoid rubbing the wrists together. With this concentration, less is more — the scent will continue to bloom on the skin for hours.
