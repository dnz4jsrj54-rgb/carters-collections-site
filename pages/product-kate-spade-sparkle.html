The House

Founded in New York City in 1993, Kate Spade has always made clothing and accessories for women who want a little color and a little wit on a Monday morning. Sparkle, composed by perfumer Louise Turner, brings that same energy to fragrance — playful, polished, and entirely unafraid to be feminine.

How to wear

Two sprays to the inside of the wrists and one to the base of the neck. Sparkle blooms on warm skin, so apply after your moisturizer and let it settle for a minute before getting dressed. It plays beautifully under a wool coat in cooler months and softens to a creamy vanilla skin scent in summer heat.
