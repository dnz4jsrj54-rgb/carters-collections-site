The House

Christian Dior introduced the original Miss Dior in 1947, naming it after his sister Catherine. The fragrance has been reformulated several times across the decades; this most recent EDP, by François Demachy, is its modern-classic interpretation.

How to wear

Three sprays — the inside of each wrist and the base of the neck. Miss Dior is moderate-projecting and rewards generosity.
