The House

Giorgio Armani founded his Milan house in 1975, defining a quietly modern Italian luxury. Armani Beauty, founded in 2000, has produced some of the most successful designer fragrances of the modern era — including the Acqua di Giò and Stronger With You lines. Stronger With You Intensely is the warmest, most gourmand expression of the family.

How to wear

Spray two to three times to clean, lightly moisturized pulse points — the inside of the wrists, the base of the neck, behind the ears. Layer over an unscented body lotion for longer wear. Avoid rubbing the wrists together; let the heat of the skin lift the heart notes on its own.
