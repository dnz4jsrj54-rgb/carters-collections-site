The House

Yves Saint Laurent founded his Paris couture house in 1961, defining a modern, liberated, fundamentally Parisian style. YSL Beauty has produced some of the most influential fragrances of the modern era — Opium, Black Opium, and now Libre. Released in 2019, Libre has become the bestselling YSL women's fragrance in less than five years.

How to wear

Spray two to three times to clean, lightly moisturized pulse points — the inside of the wrists, the base of the neck, behind the ears. Layer over an unscented body lotion for longer wear. Avoid rubbing the wrists together; let the heat of the skin lift the heart notes on its own.
