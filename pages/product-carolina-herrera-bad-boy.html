The House

Carolina Herrera built her house on Manhattan elegance — and her men's fragrances have evolved into some of the most distinctive bottles in modern designer perfumery. Bad Boy, launched in 2019, brought a gourmand edge to the masculine wardrobe and hasn't lost its grip on TikTok since.

Three sprays — chest, neck, one wrist. The peppery opening calms within twenty minutes; the cacao-tonka base is what people lean in to ask about. Excellent layered with vanilla-leaning fragrances for date nights.
