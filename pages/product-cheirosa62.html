The House

Sol de Janeiro was founded in 2015 as a New York-based homage to the warmth and joy of the Brazilian beach lifestyle. The Cheirosa 62 family — the brand's signature scent — has become one of the most viral fragrance lines of the past decade, with the body mist alone responsible for tens of millions of social impressions per year.

How to wear

Spray two to three times to clean, lightly moisturized pulse points — the inside of the wrists, the base of the neck, behind the ears. Layer over an unscented body lotion for longer wear. Avoid rubbing the wrists together; let the heat of the skin lift the heart notes on its own.
