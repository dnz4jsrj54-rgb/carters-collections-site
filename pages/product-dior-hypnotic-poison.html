The House

Dior launched Hypnotic Poison in 1998 — designed by Annick Ménardo as the seductive sister to the original Poison. It's been quietly building a cult following ever since, and TikTok's recent rediscovery has made it one of the most-requested bottles of 2026.

How to wear

Two sprays — one to the chest, one to the back of the neck. Hypnotic Poison performs best on warm skin; it bloomed beautifully throughout the day. Reapply mid-evening for a stronger sillage.
