The House

Christian Dior introduced Eau Sauvage in 1966 and Sauvage in 2015 — same name, different fragrance, fifty years apart. The 2015 release, designed by François Demachy, became the fastest-growing men's fragrance launch in industry history. The EDP version followed in 2018.

How to wear

Two sprays is plenty. Sauvage projects — three sprays is closer to four. Save the third for cold weather or evenings out.
