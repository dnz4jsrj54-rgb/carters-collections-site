The House

Lattafa is a Sharjah-based perfume house celebrated for delivering serious Middle-Eastern perfumery at honest prices. Khamrah, launched in 2022, became one of the most viral fragrances of the modern TikTok era — a warm, dates-and-vanilla gourmand that built a global cult following in under twelve months.

How to wear

Spray two to three times to clean, lightly moisturized pulse points — the inside of the wrists, the base of the neck, behind the ears. Layer over an unscented body lotion for longer wear. Avoid rubbing the wrists together; let the heat of the skin lift the heart notes on its own.
