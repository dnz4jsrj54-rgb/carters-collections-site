The House

Juliette Has a Gun approaches modern perfumery with a playful point of view. Not a Perfume became a signature of the house by putting a single fragrance material in the leading role.

How to wear

Spray directly on pulse points and allow it to air dry. It is easy for daily wear and useful for layering with floral, vanilla, citrus, or woody scents.
