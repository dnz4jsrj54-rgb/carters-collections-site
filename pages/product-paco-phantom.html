The House

Paco Rabanne, founded in 1966 by the Spanish-French couturier, has always brought a futurist sensibility to fashion and fragrance. Phantom, launched in 2021, is a collaboration with the perfumer Anne Flipo and an industry-first NFC-enabled bottle.

How to wear

Three sprays — chest, neck, one wrist. Phantom is moderate-projecting; if you want it loud, four sprays. The vanilla base lingers on clothing for days.
