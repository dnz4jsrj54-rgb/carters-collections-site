The House

Kayali was founded in 2018 by Mona Kattan — the perfumer sister behind Huda Beauty — with a focus on layerable, gender-neutral compositions. Vanilla 28 is the house's flagship and the bottle that turned Kayali from beauty-influencer brand into a serious fragrance house.

How to wear

Three sprays — chest, neck, hair. Vanilla 28 layers beautifully with citrus or oud-leaning fragrances. The brown sugar heart blooms within twenty minutes; the dry-down is where the magic settles.
