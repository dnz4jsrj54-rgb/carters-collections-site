The House

Rasasi is one of the largest perfume houses in the United Arab Emirates, founded in 1979. Their hallmark has always been quality at the price — Hawas, in particular, is a fragrance many people end up wearing more often than the bottles three times its cost.

How to wear

Three sprays to chest and neck. Hawas is generous; if you're new to it, start with two and add the third only if the room doesn't notice.
