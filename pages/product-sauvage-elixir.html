The House

Christian Dior opened his Paris couture house in 1947. The fragrance arm, founded the same year with Miss Dior, has been responsible for some of the most influential perfumes of the modern era. Sauvage Elixir, composed by perfumer François Demachy, is the line's most concentrated and most distinctive bottle.

How to wear

Spray two to three times to clean, lightly moisturized pulse points — the inside of the wrists, the base of the neck, behind the ears. Layer over an unscented body lotion for longer wear. Avoid rubbing the wrists together; let the heat of the skin lift the heart notes on its own.
