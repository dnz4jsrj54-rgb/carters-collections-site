The House

Tom Ford launched his Private Blend collection in 2007 with twelve fragrances — Tobacco Vanille was one of them, and it's the bottle that arguably built the modern luxury-gourmand category. Two decades later, it's still the standard everyone else is measured against.

How to wear

One spray — chest. That's it. Tobacco Vanille is famously potent; over-spraying will fill a room. Wear it close to the skin and let it bloom slowly. Pairs beautifully with cashmere and wool.
