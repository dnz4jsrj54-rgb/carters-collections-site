The House

Released in 2018 under Luxe Brands, Cloud became Ariana Grande's first major fragrance success and has remained a Sephora and Ulta bestseller every year since. Its quiet, comfortable sweetness — coconut, praline, lavender — defined the modern celebrity gourmand category.

How to wear

Spray two to three times to clean, lightly moisturized pulse points — the inside of the wrists, the base of the neck, behind the ears. Layer over an unscented body lotion for longer wear. Avoid rubbing the wrists together; let the heat of the skin lift the heart notes on its own.
