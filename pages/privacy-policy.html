Your Data

Privacy Policy

Plain-English account of what we collect, what we don't, who we share it with, and the rights you have over your data.

Who we are

Carter's Collections is an independent online boutique based in Austin, Texas. This policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, and the choices available to you. Privacy rights vary by location; contact us to exercise any rights that apply to you.

What we collect

  • Information you give us: Name, email address, shipping address, billing address, and phone number when you place an order or sign up for our newsletter.
  • Payment information: Processed securely by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number — only the last four digits and card brand for receipts.
  • Automatic information: IP address, browser type, pages viewed, referral source, and shopping events through Google Analytics 4, Pinterest Tag, and Meta Pixel. These tools help us understand site usage and measure advertising performance.
  • Cookies: Essential cookies (cart, checkout, session) and analytics or advertising cookies. You can disable non-essential cookies in your browser settings and manage advertising preferences through the applicable platform.
  • Optional post-purchase reviews: After checkout, Google Customer Reviews may offer you the choice to receive a survey. If you opt in, Google receives your email address, order number, delivery country, estimated delivery date, and applicable transaction information so it can send and operate the survey.

How we use it

  • To process and ship your orders
  • To send order confirmations, shipping updates, and customer service replies
  • To send marketing emails (only if you opt in — unsubscribe link in every email)
  • To improve our site and product selection based on aggregated usage data
  • To prevent fraud and comply with legal obligations

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with service providers required to fulfill your order:

  • Stripe — payment processing
  • USPS, UPS, DHL — shipping carriers (name and address only)
  • Netlify — site hosting
  • Google Analytics — anonymized usage data
  • Google Customer Reviews — transaction information for an optional post-purchase survey when you consent to the survey invitation
  • Meta and Pinterest — advertising measurement, attribution, and audience insights

Google Customer Reviews and cookies

Google and other service providers may place or read cookies, or use web beacons and similar technologies, in connection with Google Customer Reviews. Google may receive information about transactions completed on this site. The survey invitation is optional, and Google will only send the review email after you choose to opt in. You can manage or block cookies through your browser settings; blocking cookies may affect some site or survey features.

Your rights

You may request to:

  • See what personal information we have about you
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete your information (subject to legal record-keeping requirements)
  • Opt out of marketing emails at any time
  • Receive a copy of your data in a portable format where applicable

Send any request to support@carterscollections.com and we'll respond within 30 days.

Data retention

Order records are kept for 7 years for tax and accounting purposes. Marketing list data is kept until you unsubscribe. Anonymized analytics data is retained for 26 months in Google Analytics.

Children

Our site is intended for customers age 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we'll change the "last updated" date below and post the new version here. Material changes will be announced via email to subscribers.
